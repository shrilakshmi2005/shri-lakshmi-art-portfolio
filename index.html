Meet the artist

Shri Lakshmi G.

An artist driven by curiosity, patience, and the endlessly rewarding act of creating. Her work moves between delicate pen patterns and expressive painted worlds—exploring landscapes, night skies, silhouettes, mandalas, abstract forms, and the little details that make a piece feel alive.

Her signature style is a meeting of precision and play: layered strokes, rhythmic motifs, unexpected colour pairings, fine textures, and compositions that invite a closer look. Each artwork is an experiment, each collection a stepping stone toward a wider creative universe.

Still creating. Still learning. Always making.
